About Me

Building the Future, One Website at a Time

I'm Eugene, a passionate digital builder based in Ghana with a deep love for creating purposeful, high-quality digital experiences. I started Build with Eugene (BWE) to bring together design, development and strategy under one roof.

Every project I work on starts with a simple question: What does this business truly need to grow online? That question drives every design decision, every line of code and every conversation I have with clients.

Whether you're a startup looking to make your first impression or an established brand ready for a digital upgrade, I'm here to build something meaningful with you.

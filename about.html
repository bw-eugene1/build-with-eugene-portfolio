About Me

Hi, I'm Eugene

The Builder Behind BWE

I'm Eugene — a digital builder, developer and designer based in Ghana with a genuine passion for creating things that work beautifully on the internet. Build with Eugene (BWE) is more than a freelance business; it's a commitment to helping people and organisations unlock their potential through thoughtful digital solutions.

I started this journey driven by curiosity and a desire to solve real problems. I noticed that many individuals and businesses — especially in Ghana and across Africa — had brilliant ideas but lacked the digital presence to share them with the world. That gap became my purpose. BWE exists to bridge it.

My approach is straightforward: I listen first. Every project begins with understanding what you actually need — not just what you want built, but why you need it and who it's for. That understanding shapes everything from the design choices to the technical decisions. The result is work that makes sense for your audience and your goals.

I work primarily with WordPress, WooCommerce, MySQL and Python — tools that I've chosen because they're powerful, proven and right for the kinds of problems my clients face. I'm always learning, always exploring, and always looking for better ways to build.

When you work with BWE, you're not just getting a service — you're getting a builder who genuinely cares about your success and will be honest with you about what it takes to achieve it.

My Approach

What Drives the Work

The principles I hold to in every project, from start to finish

User-First Design

Every design decision starts with the person who will use it. I build intuitive, accessible interfaces that feel natural and purposeful — because great design should get out of the way and let people do what they came to do.

Clean Code

I write code that's readable, maintainable and efficient. Good code isn't just about making something work today — it's about making sure it still works, and can be improved, months and years from now.

Continuous Learning

The digital landscape evolves fast, and I evolve with it. I'm committed to staying current with best practices, emerging tools and new ideas — not just for my own growth, but because my clients deserve work built on up-to-date knowledge.
